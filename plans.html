FAQ

Good to know.

How much is EKON CRM?+
EKON CRM is $125/month plus a one-time $350 setup & build. With yearly billing it's $1,250/year (saves $250 a year) — the $350 setup still applies.
What is EKON Operator?+
EKON Operator is for wholesale teams: everything in EKON CRM plus the Operator Command Center, VA / Acquisitions / Dispositions scorecards, speed-to-lead and speed-to-offer tracking, SLA enforcement, offer and contract aging, lead routing, advanced attribution, and owner reporting. $297/month + $750 setup, or $2,997/year with $0 setup. Coming soon.
What is EKON Operator AI?+
EKON Operator AI is EKON Operator with AI working inside every department — Screening, Acquisitions, Follow Up, Dispositions, and management intelligence. AI helps screeners with reply triage and overflow, gives reps an Acquisitions Copilot and seller summaries, powers Adaptive Follow Up and database revival, and gives managers pipeline intelligence, call QA, team alerts, and a daily owner brief. Dispositions AI and call intelligence are planned. It augments your team — it doesn't replace it. $497/month + $1,000 setup, or $4,997/year with $0 setup. In early access.
Can I add EKON AI without upgrading to Operator?+
Yes. EKON AI can be added to regular EKON CRM: +$197/month + $500 AI setup, or $1,997/year with $0 AI setup. It creates the dedicated SMS + AI workspace where your screeners and EKON AI work together, while Acquisitions, Follow Up, and Dispositions stay together in Operations. If you're new to EKON, the $350 CRM setup & build still applies. In early access — not available for purchase yet.
Does AI replace my VA / screener / Acquisitions rep?+
No. EKON AI is built to work alongside your team. It can help sort replies, cover overflow, continue approved conversations, organize seller context, recover stalled leads, and personalize Follow Up. Your people stay in control and can take over whenever needed. AI doesn't set offer amounts or sign contracts.
Does Operator include AI?+
No. EKON Operator is the team-management plan. EKON Operator AI includes AI. You can also add EKON AI to the base EKON CRM plan on its own.
What happens to setup fees with annual billing?+
EKON CRM yearly still has the $350 setup & build. Operator yearly and Operator AI yearly have $0 setup. The yearly EKON AI add-on has $0 AI setup.
Do I get multiple workspaces?+
Not by default. EKON CRM runs in one all-in-one workspace — SMS, Acquisitions, Follow Up, and Dispositions together. Adding EKON AI creates 2 connected workspaces: an SMS + AI workspace where screeners and EKON AI work side-by-side, and an Operations workspace where Acquisitions, Follow Up, and Dispositions stay together. EKON Operator and Operator AI run on up to three dedicated department workspaces: SMS / Marketing, Acquisitions + Follow Up, and Dispositions.
Can I upgrade later?+
Yes. Most teams start on EKON CRM and add EKON AI or move to Operator as the operation grows.
What support is included?+
Every plan includes the Private EKON Community, EKON Support, onboarding, and training / SOP resources. Operator adds priority support, team implementation, and manager training. Operator AI adds dedicated AI onboarding, AI implementation support, priority optimization support, and dedicated account support.
Can I cancel monthly plans?+
Yes. Monthly plans are month-to-month, subject to our Terms. You keep access through the end of the period you've paid for.
How does SMS / calling usage work?+
Usage is pay-as-you-go on every plan: $0.0096 per SMS segment and $0.0166 per calling minute, with no bundled credits. Email sending is included.
