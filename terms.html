02Description of Service

EKON CRM is a done-for-you customer relationship management platform built for real estate wholesalers, powered by GoHighLevel. The service includes:

  • A configured CRM with seller pipelines (positive replies, acquisitions, follow-up, and dispositions) and lead management.
  • Bulk SMS, email, and calling tools, including a power dialer with automated call dispositions.
  • Automated follow-up and nurture sequences.
  • A seller lead capture website, KPI dashboard, and cash buyer database.
  • A2P 10DLC registration setup and onboarding.
  • Optional EKON AI features, where enabled for your account (see Section 03).

08Usage-Based Charges

These rates reflect underlying carrier and provider costs and may change if those costs change. You are responsible for maintaining a sufficient balance to send communications.
