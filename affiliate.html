Questions

Affiliate FAQ.

How much do I earn per referral?+
30% of the plan price every month: $37.50/mo for each active EKON CRM referral ($125/month plan), or $450/year per retained referral.
Do other plans pay commission?+
Right now, commission is paid on the $125/month EKON CRM plan. Commission for EKON Operator, EKON Operator AI, annual billing, and the EKON AI add-on hasn't been set yet — we'll announce it to affiliates before it applies.
Is the commission really recurring?+
Yes. It's not a one-time payout — you're paid every month the referral remains active. Five active referrals is $187.50/mo, every month.
Is there a cap on referrals?+
No cap and no expiration. Refer as many people as you want — the more active subscribers under you, the more you earn each month.
How and when do I get paid?+
Commissions are paid out monthly. We'll cover exact payout details and your referral link when you're approved into the program.
How do I join?+
Hit Apply now, fill out the quick application, and join our Discord. We review every application and typically respond within 48 hours with your link and next steps.
